Fine-resolution land-use segmentation and synthetic image–label augmentation in the Kathmandu Valley
Pranil Parajuli*, Sugam Dahal*, Kiran Chandra Dahal
* Equal contribution
Paper (arXiv) | Code & Checkpoints (GitHub)
  • Dataset and benchmark: Developed a six-class land-use segmentation benchmark covering 202.4 km² of Kathmandu Valley across 408 hand-annotated fine-resolution scenes, including predefined folds, metric records, and trained model checkpoints. The dataset is deposited on Zenodo and available by request.
  • Spatial evaluation: Compared U-Net, UNetFormer, SegFormer-B0, SegNeXt-T, and TC-SegFormer across 30 fold-runs. Sequence-block partitioning reduced mIoU by 0.051–0.095 compared with random splitting, demonstrating substantial spatial leakage.
  • Thin-structure segmentation: Developed TC-SegFormer by extending SegFormer-B0 with a half-resolution detail path, boundary-reliability reweighting, soft-clDice topology loss, and presence-balanced sampling. With only 1.9% more parameters, it improved mIoU by 0.053–0.111 across all six Kathmandu fold-runs.
  • Synthetic augmentation: Generated image–label pairs using mask diffusion and LoRA-adapted Stable Diffusion with class-channel ControlNet conditioning. Under matched compute, a 1:1 real–synthetic mixture reduced mIoU across all five architectures by 0.014 on average, with the largest losses affecting roads and rivers.

Research Experience

Kathmandu Valley Satellite Image Segmentation
2025
Python · PyTorch · SegFormer · Stable Diffusion · ControlNet · Hugging Face · CVAT

Created a 1,224-pair hand-annotated satellite dataset of Kathmandu Valley across seven land-use classes. Developed TC-SegFormer, a custom SegFormer variant combining CSA reweighting and topology-aware soft-clDice loss, improving validation mIoU from 0.466 to 0.558. Built a ControlNet-guided Stable Diffusion pipeline to generate synthetic satellite image–mask pairs targeting underrepresented land-use classes, and evaluated their impact on downstream semantic segmentation performance. Benchmarked five segmentation architectures using spatial block and random splits with three-fold cross-validation.

ControlNet + Stable Diffusion, Smart City Generation
2025
Python · Stable Diffusion · ControlNet · Diffusers · PyTorch · Hugging Face

Built a generative pipeline combining ControlNet and Stable Diffusion to synthesize realistic smart-city satellite imagery conditioned on segmentation masks and text prompts. The system supports configurable inference with DPM++2M and Euler schedulers, LoRA weights, 2× upscaling, batch processing, and a low-VRAM mode for constrained GPUs, alongside a spatial coverage analysis module and CLI documentation for usage.
